Privacy Policy

{{ updated }}

Sapumal Theatre is committed to protecting the privacy of our patrons and staff. This policy explains what information we collect through our booking systems and how we use it.

1. Information We Collect

When you book tickets or register an account, we may collect:

  • Your name, email address and phone number.
  • Booking details such as performances, seats and payment records.
  • Concession eligibility details (for example, a NIC or passport number) where a discount is claimed.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To process bookings, payments and refunds.
  • To verify concession eligibility at the box office.
  • To send booking confirmations and important service updates.
  • To maintain loyalty records and improve our productions and services.

3. Data Security

Personal data is stored securely and access is restricted to authorised staff. Sensitive identification details captured for concessions are used only for verification and are handled with appropriate care.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. Data may be shared with trusted service providers (such as payment processors) only as needed to deliver our services.

5. Your Rights

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting our box office. Some records may be retained where required for financial or legal reasons.

6. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@sapumal.lk.
